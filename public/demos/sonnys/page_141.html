Check parallel alignment using straightedge and feeler gauges; do not rotate hubs. Using a torque wrench, tighten all fasteners to values in Table 1.6. The best method for measuring round body cylinders is measuring the O.D. of the tube or center section of the cylinder not including the end caps. The tie rod cylinders can be measured based on the square dimension of the body. The body dimensions can vary by manufacturer but the measurement will provide a reasonably accurate determination of the proper bore size. The stroke size is determined by measuring the fully extended rod not including the treaded section of the rod.
